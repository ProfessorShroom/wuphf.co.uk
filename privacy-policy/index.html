WUPHF Fan App

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

This page explains what the WUPHF fan app collects, why, and what happens to it. This app is an unofficial, fan-made project connected to wuphf.co.uk and is not affiliated with NBC, Universal, or the creators of The Office.

The short version: the app doesn't have accounts, doesn't run ads, doesn't use analytics or tracking, and never sells anything to anyone. The only personal data involved is the recipient's email address you choose to send a message to, and a random device ID used purely to stop the send feature being abused for spam.

What the app collects

When you use the "Send a WUPHF" feature, the following is sent to our server in order to deliver your message:

  • The recipient's email address — whatever you type in, so the message can actually be delivered.
  • The message text you write, and optionally your name if you choose to include it.
  • A random device identifier — generated on your phone the first time you install the app, stored only on your device, and used solely to count how many messages have been sent today so the daily limit can be enforced. It isn't linked to your name, your Google account, or anything else identifying.
  • Your IP address, briefly, as a standard part of any request to our server — used the same way, purely to enforce a daily sending limit and spot abuse.

What the app does not collect

  • No account creation, no login, no password.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or advertising identifiers.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, or other apps.
  • Nothing is ever sold, rented, or shared with advertisers.

How it's used

The information above is used for exactly one purpose: delivering the message you asked to send, and preventing the sending feature from being used to spam or harass people. Message content and recipient addresses are not stored long-term — a minimal log (a shortened device ID, rough IP, and the recipient's email domain only, e.g. "gmail.com") is kept briefly for spotting abuse, then discarded.

Third parties involved

Two service providers are used to actually run the sending feature:

  • Resend (privacy policy) — handles the actual delivery of the email you send.
  • Cloudflare (privacy policy) — hosts the backend that processes your request and enforces the daily send limit.

Neither is used for advertising or any purpose beyond making the send feature work.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children, and it doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If anything here changes — say, if the app ever adds new features that collect different data — this page will be updated and the "last updated" date at the top will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be sent via app@wuphf.co.uk.
