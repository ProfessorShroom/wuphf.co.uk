Closed testing — help get the app to production

The WUPHF Android app is currently in closed testing on Google Play. Before it can go live to everyone, Google requires a small group of people to opt in and test it first. That's where you come in — thank you!

The short version: join the Google Group below, then open the opt-in link on your Android phone and follow the prompts to install from Google Play. It takes a couple of minutes.

How to join

1

Join the tester Google Group. This is how Google checks you're allowed to install the test build — it needs to be done with the same Google account you'll use on your Android phone.

Join the Google Group
2

Open the opt-in link on your Android phone, using the same Google account you just joined the group with, and tap "Become a tester".

Become a Tester
3

Install the app from Google Play — the opt-in page will link you straight to the listing. It can take a little while (sometimes up to a few hours) for Google to sync your account after joining the group, so if the opt-in page doesn't work right away, give it some time and try again.

4

Test it out! Try sending a WUPHF to ryan@wuphf.com to see it in action.

Why this is here temporarily

Google requires at least 12 testers to opt in and stay active for 14 days before an app can move from closed testing to production. Once that's done, WUPHF will be publicly available on Google Play and this page (and the tester group) will no longer be needed. If you're reading this and the app is already live, thank you for the help — you can go ahead and grab it from the Play Store directly!
